ArmGPT — MODE 12 emulation

The ACORN chat client on a 1987 Acorn Archimedes, now with a DOS-style conversation menu. Click the screen and type; click a chat on the left to switch, or + New chat.

Archimedes

Press RETURN at the splash. Type and press RETURN to chat. Click a conversation to switch, + New chat to start one (Ctrl+N); Alt+↑/↓ cycles. Replies are canned (no server).
